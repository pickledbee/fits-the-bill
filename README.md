# Fits the Bill

A sit-down card party game. Pick a chair at a virtual table, serve the card that best answers the order, and tip your favorite. Most tips after 8 rounds wins.

- `index.html` is the whole game.
- `Code.gs` is the Google Apps Script that gives everyone one shared room, backed by a Google Sheet.

Without a shared room the game still plays against house players on one device.
